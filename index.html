What is this?

Not a real ticket

eventdim is a design experiment. It emulates the look of a mobile ticket screen the way an emulator recreates a console’s shell, not the thing itself. Nothing it makes is a ticket.

It isn’t affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any ticketing service, venue, or event organizer. Its QR codes encode only the words “NOT A TICKET” and a render number, so they won’t scan as valid anywhere.

Please don’t use a render to get into an event, to resell, or to suggest you hold a ticket you don’t. Event images belong to their owners.

Images you add are processed in your browser and saved only on this device.
